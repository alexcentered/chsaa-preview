The timeline

1896

Compton Union High School opens

Classes begin on September 7 in two rooms of the Compton City Grammar School. The first graduating class, in 1898, has two students.

Source: Class of 1973 history; CHSAA

  1. 1896

    Compton Union High School opens

    Classes begin on September 7 in two rooms of the Compton City Grammar School. The first graduating class, in 1898, has two students.

    Source: Class of 1973 history; CHSAA

  2. 1903

    A home at Myrrh and Acacia

    The school moves into its first building of its own. The Masonic Lodge lays the cornerstone.

    Source: Class of 1973 history

  3. 1927

    The college arrives, and the Baby Tartars

    Compton Junior College forms within the high school district. Its teams are the Tartars, and the high school students become the Baby Tartars.

    Source: Class of 1973 history; Wikipedia, Compton College

  4. 1933

    The Long Beach earthquake

    On March 10 the earthquake devastates the shared campus. Only two buildings are left standing.

    Source: Calisphere / Los Angeles Public Library; Wikipedia

  5. 1935

    The Administration Building

    The rebuilt Administration Building on Acacia becomes the landmark generations of alumni remember.

    Source: Class of 1973 history

  6. 1939

    Tarbabes, in print

    Baby Tartars becomes Tartar Babes, then Tarbabes. The name first appears in the Dar-U-Gar yearbook.

    Source: Class of 1973 history

  7. 1951

    The School of Champions

    Coach J. Kenneth Fagans' basketball teams win 53 straight games and the 1951 and 1952 CIF Southern Section titles.

    Source: CHSAA Hall of Fame

  8. 1953

    Compton Senior High School

    The college moves to Artesia Boulevard and the high school stands on its own again.

    Source: Wikipedia, Compton College

  9. 1960

    Columbia blue and white

    After red and white, maroon and gray, then blue and grey, the school settles on Columbia blue and white.

    CHSAA's history page gives 1953 for this change. Pending Board confirmation.

    Source: Class of 1973 history

  10. 2007

    The sword is passed

    CHSAA incorporates on October 26, 2006. At the first Compton High School Alumni Day, May 15, 2007, the Tarbabe Task Force passes a ceremonial sword to the new association.

    Source: CHSAA

  11. 2025

    A new campus

    The rebuilt Compton High School opens in May 2025, including a performing arts center funded by a $10 million gift. The first Homecoming on the new campus ends in a 38 to 21 win over Long Beach Jordan.

    Source: LAist, May 2025; Compton Bulletin, Oct 2025
